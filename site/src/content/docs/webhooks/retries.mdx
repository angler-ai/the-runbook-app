Each matching subscription gets its own **delivery** of an event
(`whd_…`). Deliveries are claimed under a lease, `POST`ed with no database
transaction open, and then recorded, so a crash between the receiver's
answer and the record means the same body, event ID and delivery ID are sent
again. That's why receivers deduplicate on the event ID.

## What happens to an attempt

| The receiver | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Answers `2xx` within 10 seconds | **Delivered** |
| Times out, refuses the connection, or answers `5xx`, `408` or `429` | **Retried** with backoff |
| Answers anything else (`3xx`, other `4xx`) | **Failed** at once, not retried |
| Resolves to an address it may not reach (private, loopback, link-local) | **Failed** at once |

Redirects are never followed: a `3xx` is a failure.

## Backoff

The wait before the next attempt starts at 30 seconds and doubles, up to
two hours:

Retries stop **24 hours** after the delivery was created; a delivery still
failing then ends as `retries_exhausted`. The exact schedule is in the
[event reference](/reference/webhook-events/#delivery-rules), generated from
the server's code.

## Redelivery

`runbook webhook redeliver <whd> --webhook <whk> --project <p>` sends a
delivery's event again under a **new delivery ID**, with a fresh 24-hour
retry window. The event ID stays the same, so a receiver that already
handled it skips it.

## Failing subscriptions

A subscription whose deliveries fail continuously for **three days** is
disabled. The subscription shows a notice for project admins, and
deliveries stop until someone fixes the receiver and re-enables it:

```sh
runbook webhook update whk_… --project prod --enable
```

Enabling resets its delivery health. Quiet gaps don't end a failing streak
unless they're longer than two retry windows (48 hours).

## Events for people

`approval.requested`, `approval.escalated`, `approval.expired`,
`step.blocked`, `step.reminder` and `step.escalated` are notices for people.
When one reaches no subscriber at all (none subscribed, or every delivery
failed), the run page says so, so nobody waits on a message that was never
sent. Email and the in-app inbox carry the same notices.

<Aside type="note">
Delivery logs are kept for 90 days and then purged. The run's own record
is unaffected.
</Aside>
